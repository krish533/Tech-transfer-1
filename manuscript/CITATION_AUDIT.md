# Citation audit (October 2026)

Each reference added in the October 2026 revision was checked for bibliographic details and for whether it supports the sentence that cites it. Two sentences were corrected.

## Corrected

| Reference | Issue | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Ouellette and Tutt (2020) | The article reports that the earlier finding that higher inventor royalties raise licensing income was driven by coding errors. "Sensitive to how policies are coded" understated this. | Introduction now says the reported link "does not survive corrections to how those shares were coded." |
| Jensen, Thursby and Thursby (2003) | Cited for "invention disclosure is widely regarded as incomplete." The paper models *whether and at what stage* faculty disclose; it does not report an overall disclosure rate. | Discussion now says faculty "choose whether, and at what stage, to disclose inventions." |
| Choi, Gulati and Posner (2013) | Ending page could not be confirmed. | Entry now gives 88 N.Y.U. L. Rev. 1 (2013), matching the University of Chicago repository citation. |
